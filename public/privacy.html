Privacy information

These landing pages are operated by WesTec Roofing. Updated September 30, 2026.

Your assessment request

When you submit a form, we collect your name, email address, phone number, home address, and the roofing service you requested. We use this information to respond to your request, arrange an assessment, and discuss your roofing project by phone, text, or email.

Submissions are stored using Cloudflare and sent to our Monday.com lead management board. We also store available campaign and ad identifiers from the page address to understand where requests came from. For abuse prevention, the form uses a hashed version of your IP address with a private salt and a short time window to limit repeated submissions.

Advertising measurement

When enabled, the Meta Pixel sends page-visit and successful form-submission events to Meta. These events help measure advertising and include the page visited, the roofing service, and a submission reference. Meta's technology may use cookies and collect browser, device, and network information. Our event code does not attach your name, email, phone number, or home address as event parameters.

These pages skip loading the Pixel when your browser sends a Global Privacy Control signal. Browser privacy settings and tracking blockers may also limit tracking. See Meta's privacy policy for information about Meta's processing.
